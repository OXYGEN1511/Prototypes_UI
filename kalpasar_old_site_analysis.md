## About Us Content (from /about-us)

**The Project:**
The Gulf of Khambhat Development Project is mainly a water resources project involving creation of a fresh water reservoir in the Gulf of Khambhat for meeting demand of irrigation, domestic and industrial water supply.

**Key Stats:**
- Gujarat = 6.4% of India's land, 5% of population, only **2% of surface water**
- Per capita water: 990 m³/yr (below minimum 1,700 m³/yr)
- Gulf stretches ~200 km N-S, 25–150 km wide
- Area enclosed: ~2,000 sq km (of 17,000 sq km total)
- Dam will store ~**10,000 million cubic meters** of water from Narmada, Dhadhar, Mahi, Sabarmati & Saurashtra rivers
